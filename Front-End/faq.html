Frequently Asked Questions

Subscriptions & delivery

How often will I get water?
Choose weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly when you create the subscription. You can change the frequency or pause anytime from the Subscriptions page.

Can I skip a delivery?
Yes — pause the subscription before the next delivery date. We won't auto-renew while it's paused.

What if no one's home?
Our driver leaves the order at the address on file. For office accounts we coordinate a drop-off window in the order notes.

Payments

What payment methods do you accept?
Major credit and debit cards via Square. Card details never touch Sparko's servers — Square tokenizes them in the browser.

How does auto-renewal billing work?
On the renewal date, the subscription Lambda creates a new order and charges your saved card. You'll get an email receipt.

Can I get a refund?
Yes — email support within 7 days. We'll refund through Square; the credit shows on your statement in 3-5 business days.

Rewards

How do I earn points?
Every dollar spent earns 10 points. Posting a product review earns 25 points.

How do I redeem points?
Enter the number of points to redeem in the cart sidebar at checkout. 100 points = $1 of discount.

Do points expire?
Not yet. We may add an expiration policy if abuse becomes a problem; you'd be notified in advance.

What are tiers?
Lifetime points determine your tier: bronze (0+), silver (500+), gold (2000+), platinum (5000+). Tier perks are coming soon.

Account & security

I forgot my password.
Use the password reset page; we'll email you a one-hour link.

How is my data protected?
Passwords are hashed with PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA512. AWS-to-AWS traffic uses TLS 1.2+; browser-to-Sparko traffic is HTTPS only when a domain is configured (this demo is HTTP-only). Card data is handled entirely by Square (PCI-compliant). See our privacy policy for details.

Can I delete my account?
Email support with your request and we'll handle it within 30 days, in line with our privacy policy.
