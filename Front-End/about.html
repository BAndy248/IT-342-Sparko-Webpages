About Sparko

Sparko Water is a campus-scale subscription water delivery service built as the capstone for IT-342 cloud development at Cebu Institute of Technology – University. The platform demonstrates a full multi-tier AWS architecture: Auto Scaling Groups behind an Application Load Balancer, RDS Multi-AZ for the database, Lambda for scheduled subscription renewals, and CloudFront for static asset delivery.

What we deliver

  • Natural spring, purified, alkaline, and sparkling water in multiple sizes
  • Weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly delivery subscriptions
  • One-off orders through the shopping cart
  • Bundle discounts and a rewards-points program

How it works

  • Browse the catalog and add items to your cart.
  • Eligible bundles ("Buy 6, save 10%") are applied automatically at checkout.
  • Every dollar spent earns 10 reward points. 100 points = $1 to redeem on a future order.
  • Active subscriptions auto-renew on schedule and charge your saved payment method.
