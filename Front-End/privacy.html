Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-12. Class-project policy — not legal advice.

What we collect

  • Account info: username, email, name, phone number.
  • Delivery info: street, city, state, ZIP code.
  • Order history: products purchased, dates, quantities, totals.
  • Subscription state: plan, frequency, next delivery.
  • Rewards activity: points earned and redeemed.
  • Reviews: ratings and comments you write are public, with your username.
  • Operational data: request logs (IP, user agent, request id) for security and debugging, retained 30 days in CloudWatch.

We do not collect or store full payment card details. Card data is tokenized by Square in the browser; Sparko only sees Square's opaque payment id.

How we use your information

  • To deliver your orders and renew subscriptions.
  • To respond to support requests.
  • To send transactional emails (password reset, order confirmation, renewal receipt).
  • To detect and prevent fraud.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Who we share with

  • AWS hosts our infrastructure, database, and logs.
  • Square processes payments and stores card details.
  • SES (Amazon Simple Email Service) sends transactional email.

Cookies and tracking

We use a single browser storage entry (sparko_token) to keep you signed in between visits. We don't use third-party advertising trackers.

Security

  • Passwords are stored as PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA512 hashes with 210,000 iterations.
  • Database storage is encrypted at rest (AWS KMS).
  • Traffic to AWS services (RDS, Secrets Manager, S3) is encrypted with TLS 1.2+. Browser-to-Sparko traffic uses TLS 1.2+ when a domain + TLS certificate are configured; this class-demo deployment runs over plain HTTP and should not be used with real customer data.
  • Database access is limited to private VPC subnets and a strict security-group chain.

Your rights

  • Access: see your profile, addresses, orders, and rewards in your account.
  • Correction: update your profile from the Profile page.
  • Deletion: email support@sparkowater.example.com to delete your account; we'll process the request within 30 days.
  • Export: ask support and we'll send you a JSON dump of your data.

Children's privacy

Sparko is not directed to anyone under 18 and we don't knowingly collect data from minors.

Changes

We'll post updates here with a revised "last updated" date.
